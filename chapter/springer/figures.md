# Figures for the Springer chapter

Figure files are in `outputs/sepsis/figures/` (PNG for the Word file, PDF for the publisher). Each
figure is placed after the paragraph that first cites it. Captions are rewritten section by section.

- **Fig. 1** (`fig_workflow`). The CAB procedure. Before any local label is examined, the hospital fixes its requirements and error level. Stage 1 tests the imported model on all labelled patients; Stage 2, reached only if Stage 1 certifies nothing, tests a model adapted on the fit half using the certification half. The first configuration certified is deployed; otherwise the model is blocked.
- **Fig. 2** (`fig1_transfer`). Local and imported model at each receiving hospital: hourly AUROC, normalised utility, patient-level sensitivity and the share of non-septic patients alerted, with 95% patient-bootstrap confidence intervals, on the receiving hospital's test patients.
- **Fig. 3** (`fig2_cab_decisions`). What CAB deployed, by the number of labelled patients, in each whole-hospital transfer and in the same-hospital control, at the primary requirements (100 runs per budget). The correct remedy, an evaluation reference computed from the receiving hospital's full data, is shown above each panel.
- **Fig. 4** (`fig3_unsafe`). Share of runs in which each policy deployed an unsafe configuration, by policy and number of labelled patients, at the primary requirements (100 runs per budget). The dashed line marks the 5% level that CAB's guarantee bounds. Some lines coincide: As-is, Label-free and Recalibrate for hospital A's model at B; As-is, Label-free, Recalibrate and Retrain, and also Fine-tune and Closed test, for hospital B's model at A; and As-is and Label-free in hospital B's control.
- **Fig. 5** (`fig5_baselines`). CAB against practical alternatives: share of runs that deployed an unsafe configuration and share that chose the correct remedy, averaged over all requirement pairs and budgets (100 runs per setting), for the whole-hospital transfers and the same-hospital control.
- **Fig. 6** (`fig_labels_tolerance`). What certification costs, and what changes it. (a) Septic patients needed to certify sensitivity, by the model's true margin over the target (calculator at a fixed threshold; power 0.9, α/4 per test). (b, c) Share of samples in which CAB approved the unchanged model (KEEP or CALIBRATE) in the same-hospital control, by the hospital's false-alert tolerance, with the models held fixed and labelled samples drawn with replacement from the test patients (200 samples per point).
- **Fig. 7** (`fig_app_gate`). The deployment gate of the prototype application, installing hospital A's model at hospital B with 12,000 randomly labelled patients (requirements: sensitivity at least 80%, non-septic patients alerted at most 45.1%): the decision, the evidence for each option in order of intervention (counts, exact one-sided bounds at α/4 and the result), and the retrospective simulation check on the 8,000 patients the gate did not see.
- **Fig. 8** (`fig_app_ward`). The ward monitor, replaying hospital B's test patients at hour 24 with the adapted model certified in Fig. 7: the alerting system in use, the ward table with each patient's score relative to the threshold, alert status and 24-hour trend, and one patient's risk trajectory against the alert threshold.
